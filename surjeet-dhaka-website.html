About

Dr. Surjeet Singh Dhaka is an Assistant Professor of Agribusiness at the Central University of Punjab, Bathinda, teaching in the M.B.A. (Agribusiness) programme since January 2020. He holds a Ph.D. and M.B.A. in Agribusiness from the Institute of Agri Business Management, Swami Keshwanand Rajasthan Agricultural University, Bikaner, and a B.Sc. (Hons) in Horticulture.

His work spans agricultural price forecasting, agripreneurship, consumer behaviour in food systems and the socioeconomics of climate-resilient and smart farming. He has also worked in industry (sales and marketing, fertiliser) and in market-intelligence research under a national network project.
